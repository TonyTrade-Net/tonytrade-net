## The three things it does best

- **AI chat:** ask "What's the structure on BTC right now?" – the AI reads the candles across timeframes and answers in plain words.
- **AI levels on your chart:** click any price or time in the AI's answer and it's marked on the chart; select a stretch of candles and ask the AI about just that part. Plus 37 drawing tools for your own support, resistance and trend lines.
- **Strategy plans:** say your idea the way you'd say it – the AI turns it into a plan with entry, stop and expiry, and you go through it line by line.

## Everything else

- **Charts:** up to four linked charts on one screen, candle replay, coin overlays, large trades marked.
- **Condition scanner:** screen the whole market across several timeframes and pass results straight to the AI.
- **Backtests:** run your plan over past prices with ready-made strategy templates.
- **Paper trading:** real market data, virtual funds, no exchange key needed.
- **Alerts:** sharp moves and your own conditions, with sound, system notifications and Telegram.
- **Daily and weekly summaries:** a roundup of the market, your watchlist and your AI plans.

The current download does not place orders. The AI drafts plans; it never gets your keys.

Not for the current version. Charts, scans, AI analysis and paper trading need no key.

**Can TonyTrade withdraw my funds?**
No. Your funds stay in your own exchange account, and TonyTrade has no transfer feature.
